**Quick answer:** open our free [Merge PDF](/merge-pdf/) tool, select the PDFs you want to combine, click **Create file** and download `merged.pdf`. Everything happens inside your browser, so your documents are never uploaded to a server, which matters when the files contain contracts, ID copies or financial records.

## Why people merge PDFs

Merging turns many files into one that is easier to send, store and read. Typical reasons include:

- Combining a cover letter, CV and certificates into one job application.
- Joining several invoices or receipts for an expense claim.
- Putting scanned pages from a phone app or scanner into a single document.
- Bundling chapters, contracts and annexures into one file for a client.
- Meeting a portal's "upload one PDF" rule.

## How to merge PDFs, step by step

1. Open the [Merge PDF tool](/merge-pdf/).
2. Click **Choose files** and select two or more PDF files. On most computers hold Ctrl (Windows) or Cmd (Mac) to select several at once, or tap multiple files on a phone.
3. Click **Create file**. The tool copies every page from each file into a new document.
4. When the result appears, download `merged.pdf` and open it to check that every page is present and in the right place.

No account, watermark or page limit is applied by the tool. The practical limit is your device's memory: very large files, such as hundreds of megabytes of scans, may be slow or may fail on older phones.

## Getting the order right

The pages of the merged file follow the order in which your files were selected, and within each file the pages keep their original order. Because file pickers often list files alphabetically and may add them in that order, the easiest way to control the result is to **name your files with numbers first**: `01-cover-letter.pdf`, `02-cv.pdf`, `03-certificates.pdf`. Select them together and they will usually come out in that sequence.

Always scroll through the output before sending it. If you spot a wrong order, rename and merge again; your originals remain unchanged because the tool creates a new file.

## How the merge works, and why it keeps quality

The tool uses the open-source pdf-lib library in your browser. It copies each page from the source documents into a new one, rather than converting pages to images. That means text stays selectable and searchable, images keep their quality, and vector graphics stay sharp, and the merged file is about the same size as the originals added together.

## Common problems and fixes

**"Could not process this file."** The PDF may be damaged, password-protected or use an unusual structure. Try opening it in a PDF viewer and printing it to PDF to create a clean copy, then merge that. If a file is protected and you have the right to open it, remove the protection with our [Unlock PDF](/unlock-pdf/) tool and use your password.

**The merged file is too big for an upload limit.** Merging does not shrink files. You can try the [Compress PDF](/compress-pdf/) tool afterwards, but note that it rebuilds pages as compressed images, which reduces the size at the cost of selectable text and sharpness. For documents where text must stay searchable, reduce image resolution at the scanning stage instead.

**Pages appear sideways.** Fix the orientation in a source file with [Rotate PDF](/rotate-pdf/) before merging.

**I only need a few pages.** Use [Split PDF](/split-pdf/) to extract the pages you want, then merge those pieces.

**Bookmarks or form fields are missing.** Merging copies page content, but document-level features like bookmarks and some interactive form behaviour may not carry over. If you rely on them, check the result and use a desktop PDF editor for complex forms.

## Merging scans and images

If your documents are photos or scans in JPG or PNG format, convert them to PDF first with the [Image to PDF](/image-to-pdf/) tool, then merge the resulting PDFs. For paperwork you photograph with a phone, crop and straighten each page and keep the image resolution reasonable so the final file is not enormous. See our guide on [compressing images to a target size](/blog/compress-image-to-20kb-50kb-100kb/) for tips.

## Privacy and safety

Handling confidential documents online is a risk if a tool uploads them. Our merge runs locally: the files you choose are read by your browser and the output is created on your device. Nothing is sent to us. You can confirm this by switching your device offline after the page loads and noticing that the merge still works. For very sensitive documents, also work on a device you trust and delete the downloaded file when you no longer need it. Read our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/) for the full picture.

Only merge documents you have the right to use. Combining or unlocking files you do not own or have permission for may breach copyright or confidentiality rules.

## Tips for a professional result

- **Name the final file clearly**, for example `Anita-Sharma-Application.pdf`, instead of `merged.pdf`.
- **Keep consistent page sizes.** Mixing A4 and Letter pages is allowed but can look uneven; check before printing.
- **Check the total size** against the receiving portal's limit before you try to upload.
- **Keep your originals** so you can reorder or replace a document and merge again.
- **Review every page** once, especially signatures, stamps and the last page, which are the most commonly missing items.
