## Why PDFs get so large

A PDF is a container. What makes it big is almost always what is inside it:

- **Scanned pages.** A scan is a photograph of a page. At high resolution, one colour page can take several megabytes.
- **Phone photos.** Pictures taken on a modern phone are often 3 to 8 MB each, and the PDF keeps them at that size.
- **High-resolution images.** Brochures, reports and presentations with large pictures grow quickly.
- **Many pages.** Size adds up page by page.

A document that contains only typed text is usually small, often tens of kilobytes. That is why a "simple" scanned form can be larger than a long typed report.

## The 4 ways to make a PDF smaller

| Method | Best for | What you give up |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Compress the pages (image-based) | Scanned or photo-heavy PDFs | Selectable text, some sharpness |
| 2. Shrink the images before making the PDF | Photos and scans you have not turned into a PDF yet | Nothing extra, if done at the start |
| 3. Export again with smaller image settings | PDFs you created from Word, PowerPoint or Excel | Image quality, set by you |
| 4. Remove or split pages | Files with pages you do not need | Those pages |

You can combine them. For example, split off the pages you need, then compress the result once.

## Which quality level should you pick?

- **High:** documents with small print, signatures, stamps or tables you must be able to read clearly.
- **Medium:** most everyday uses, such as emailing a scan or uploading a certificate. Start here.
- **Low:** when you must hit a strict size limit and the content is large and simple, such as a photo or a big headline page.

If the result is still too large at Low, the fix is not more compression. Reduce the pages, rescan at a lower resolution, or shrink the original images first.

## What are the usual size limits?

Limits depend entirely on where you upload or send the file, so read the instructions on the form. As a rough guide:

- **Email:** many providers limit attachments to around 20 to 25 MB in total. Gmail, for example, allows 25 MB per message.
- **Application and exam portals:** often set lower limits, such as 200 KB, 500 KB, 1 MB or 2 MB.
- **Messaging apps and forms:** vary widely, so check the message shown on the upload button.

Always confirm the file size in your file manager before uploading.

## What you lose when you compress pages as images

Image-based compression has one big side effect: **text becomes part of the picture**. After compressing:

- You cannot select or copy text.
- Search inside the PDF will not find words.
- Screen readers cannot read the page aloud.

If you need the text, keep the original file for that purpose, or extract the words with our [OCR PDF](/ocr-pdf/) tool, which reads scanned pages and gives you plain text you can copy. For documents that are mostly typed text, the original is usually already small, so compressing may not help much.

## Tips to get a smaller PDF without ruining it

1. **Scan at a sensible resolution.** Around 150 to 200 dpi is enough for reading on screen. Very high resolution adds size without helping.
2. **Use black and white for text documents.** A text page scanned in colour is much heavier than the same page in greyscale or black and white.
3. **Crop empty margins and straighten pages** before scanning or converting, so no space is wasted.
4. **Create the PDF from reduced images.** If you are turning photos into a PDF, shrink them first using the [Resize Image](/resize-image/) or [Compress Image](/compress-image/) tool, then build the PDF with [Image to PDF](/image-to-pdf/). Our guide to [compressing images to 20 KB, 50 KB or 100 KB](/blog/compress-image-to-20kb-50kb-100kb/) shows how.
5. **Remove pages you do not need.** Use [Split PDF](/split-pdf/) to keep only the pages you must send.
6. **Compress once.** Run the tool on your original file, not on an already compressed copy. Each pass loses detail.
7. **Merge first, then compress.** Joining files does not shrink them. If a combined file is too big, merge with [Merge PDF](/merge-pdf/), then compress the final file once. Our guide to [merging PDF files](/blog/how-to-merge-pdf-files/) explains the steps.

## Troubleshooting

**The file is still too big.** Use Low quality, split out unneeded pages, or rescan at a lower resolution. If it is made of many large photos, shrink those photos before building the PDF.

**The text looks blurry.** Go back to the original and compress at High or Medium. Very small print needs more quality.

**I need to send the PDF to a portal that asks for searchable text.** Do not compress it as images. Reduce the size at the source instead, for example by exporting again from Word with smaller image settings.

**The PDF has a password.** Remove the protection first using the [Unlock PDF](/unlock-pdf/) tool if you have the right to do so, then compress.

**The pages are sideways.** Fix them with [Rotate PDF](/rotate-pdf/) before compressing.
