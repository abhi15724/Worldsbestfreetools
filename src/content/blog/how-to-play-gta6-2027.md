## What platforms can you play GTA 6 on in 2027?

GTA 6 launched on **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X/S** in 2025. The **PC version** followed in 2026, which means by 2027 all three platforms are available. If you are on PS4, Xbox One or a low-end PC, GTA 6 will not run — Rockstar built this one exclusively for current-generation hardware.

Before buying, check your machine against these:

| Component | Minimum | Recommended |

If your PC does not meet minimum specs, console is the smarter purchase right now.

## How to buy GTA 6

**Console players:** buy a physical disc or digital copy from the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store. No subscription is required for the story mode.

**PC players:** GTA 6 is available on the Rockstar Games Launcher and Steam. Buy from either, but note that the Rockstar Launcher runs in the background regardless.

**Watch out for:** third-party key sites selling at a discount. Some are legitimate grey-market sellers; others sell revoked or region-locked keys. Stick to Steam, the Rockstar store or major retailers to be safe.

## Getting started: first steps

1. **Install and update first.** Day-one patches on GTA 6 are large. Connect to the internet and let it update fully before launching.
2. **Play story mode before GTA Online.** The singleplayer campaign introduces the map, mechanics and characters. Jumping straight into Online without context makes the game much harder to follow.
3. **Adjust graphics settings.** On PC especially, start on medium settings and work up. GTA 6 is demanding even on powerful hardware.
4. **Link your Rockstar Social Club account.** You need this for GTA Online and to carry progress across platforms if you ever switch.

---

## Tips to get the most out of GTA 6

- **Explore Leonida slowly.** The map is enormous. Side missions, random events and hidden areas reward players who do not rush the story.
- **GTA Online is free with the base game.** You do not need a separate purchase, though Shark Cards let you buy in-game currency.
- **Enable performance mode on console.** Most players prefer 60 fps over 4K on PS5 and Series X.

## How to choose your platform

- **I want the best performance right now:** PS5 or Xbox Series X — both run GTA 6 at 60 fps in performance mode with no setup needed.
- **I already own a powerful gaming PC:** buy on Steam or the Rockstar Launcher for mod support in the future and better long-term graphics.
- **I am on a budget:** Xbox Series S runs GTA 6 and is the cheapest current-gen option, though at lower resolution than Series X.
- **I want to play with friends:** match the platform your friends are on — GTA Online is not cross-platform.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can you play GTA 6 on PC in 2027?**
Yes. The PC version of GTA 6 released in 2026, so by 2027 it is available on PC via the Rockstar Games Launcher and Steam, alongside PS5 and Xbox Series X/S.

**What are the minimum PC requirements for GTA 6?**
You need at least an Intel i7-9700K or Ryzen 5 5600X, an RTX 3070 or RX 6800 XT, 16 GB RAM, and 150 GB of SSD storage running Windows 10 64-bit.

**Is GTA Online free with GTA 6?**
Yes. GTA Online is included with the base game purchase. You do not need a separate subscription, though Shark Cards let you buy in-game currency.

**Is GTA 6 on PS4 or Xbox One?**
No. GTA 6 is exclusive to current-generation hardware: PS5, Xbox Series X/S and PC. It will not run on last-generation consoles.
